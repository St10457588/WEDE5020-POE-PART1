Plumbing Compliance Certificates

Required by law for all property transfers in South Africa, our plumbing compliance certificates ensure your property meets SANS 10252 and SANS 10254 standards.

What's Included:

  • Comprehensive plumbing inspection
  • Testing of water pressure and flow rates
  • Verification of backflow prevention
  • Drainage system inspection
  • Issuance of Certificate of Compliance (COC)
  • Recommendations for any required repairs

Note: As PIRB registered plumbers (License No: 12345), we are authorised to issue legally binding compliance certificates.
