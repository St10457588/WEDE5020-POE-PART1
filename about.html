Our Story

Tapstone Plumbing Solutions was established in 2018 by master plumber Thabo Moloi in Randburg, Gauteng. What started as a one-man operation has grown into a trusted team of four qualified plumbers serving both Randburg and Benoni areas.

Our journey began with a simple mission: to provide reliable, compliant, and affordable plumbing solutions that protect South African homes and businesses. Today, we specialise in emergency repairs, bathroom renovations, geyser installations, and compliance certifications for property transfers.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, compliant, and affordable plumbing solutions that protect South African homes and businesses while building lasting relationships through exceptional service.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted plumbing contractor in Gauteng, recognised for quality workmanship, PIRB compliance, and customer satisfaction.
